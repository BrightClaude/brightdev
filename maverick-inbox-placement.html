The client

About the client and the problem they faced

Business size$10M–$100M
Years in business20+ years
IndustryHouseware, home furnishing & garden

Alen® is an Austin-based innovator of trusted, top-rated HEPA air purification products dedicated to helping people breathe cleaner, healthier air. With over 20 years of industry leadership, Alen has built a reputation for combining superior performance, quiet operation, and thoughtful design that fits modern homes, offices, and commercial spaces.

The challenge

Alen® faced an inbox placement issue where even high-quality campaigns weren’t consistently reaching the primary inbox. Despite strong creative and a loyal audience, many emails landed in promotions or spam, limiting visibility and ultimately leaving conversions and revenue on the table.

Results

Our wins

By enhancing inbox placement through Maverick and leveraging our lifecycle expertise, Alen transformed email into a more powerful and reliable source of revenue, all without adding manual effort or redesigning campaigns.

Here’s how we approached the project

✓

Maverick’s Inbox Placement Optimizer, across every campaign email

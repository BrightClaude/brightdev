From missed opportunities to $100K+ growth.

How fixing the core lifecycle flows for Chaparral Motorsports generated over $154K in incremental email and SMS revenue in just two months.

The client

About the client and the problem they faced

Business size$10M–$100M
Years in business46+ years
IndustryMotorcycle accessories

Chaparral Motorsports had 46 years of brand equity and a loyal powersports customer base, but their lifecycle email strategy had critical gaps. Core automations like Exit Intent, Browse Abandonment, and optimized Welcome and Cart Recovery flows were missing or underperforming, leaving high-intent customers unconverted.

The challenge

Without effective Welcome, Exit Intent, and Browse Abandonment flows in place, the brand wasn’t reaching customers at the right moments in their journey. This led to missed revenue during high-intent stages, lower conversion rates, and a significant amount of revenue left on the table.

Results

Email & SMS marketing results

In just two months, improvements across email and SMS flows drove higher deliveries and significant growth, with both channels scaling revenue and strengthening overall lifecycle performance.

  • Flow revenue: $150,539 → $252,452 (+$101.9K)
  • SMS revenue: $1,648 → $54,289 (+$52.6K)
  • Flow deliveries: 48,911 → 110,355 (+126%)
  • SMS deliveries: 3,273 → 11,525 (+8,252)

The approach

Here’s how we approached the project

We transformed Chaparral Motorsports’ lifecycle marketing strategy by filling in critical gaps and building out foundational flows. By focusing on the most impactful automations, we drove over $100K in incremental revenue.

1

Core foundational flow implementation

Key lifecycle email automations created a strong baseline system that nearly doubled the brand’s revenue.

2

Emphasized exit intent automation

Despite lower volume, prioritizing the Exit Intent flow generated some of the highest revenue per recipient across the strategy.

3

SMS augmented overall performance

Introducing SMS as a complementary channel amplified email results and scaled revenue to more than $54K from a near-zero baseline.

Takeaways

Lifecycle strategy for scalable growth

This is what lifecycle email looks like in practice: not a single campaign, but a connected system that captures revenue at every stage of the customer journey.

In two months we delivered over $154K in incremental revenue across email and SMS. Foundational flows are a foundation for consistent, scalable growth, and SMS isn’t a nice-to-have, it’s a precision channel that amplifies what email builds.

How we drove 102% email revenue growth for a leading apparel brand

The client

About the client and the problem they faced

Business size$5M–$10M
Years in business11+ years
IndustrySporting goods

This apparel brand had built a loyal customer base over 11 years, but their lifecycle email marketing strategy wasn’t converting that loyalty into repeat revenue.

After trying a different agency partner, they came back to Flowium, specifically for the structured processes and measurable results they hadn’t found elsewhere. That trust was the foundation. What happened next was the proof.

The challenge

The brand had a substantial audience but was missing opportunities to further maximize performance through consistently optimized lifecycle flows. A large share of customers purchased only once and did not return, exposing weaknesses in the post-purchase journey.

Results

102% revenue growth in 3 months

In just three months, a 102.3% increase in total email attributed revenue (from £119K to £242K), including a 164.8% increase in campaign revenue (£35K to £94K), an 80.6% increase in flow revenue (£75K to £135K), a 69.7% increase in first-time customer revenue (£71K to £121K), and a 150.7% increase in returning customer revenue (£48K to £120K).

The approach

Here’s how we approached the project

We took ownership of all flows, rebuilding the lifecycle email marketing strategy, enhancing the post-purchase experience, and optimizing each stage of the funnel to drive stronger retention and sustainable revenue growth.

1

Brand alignment & visual identity

Five initial campaign emails captured the brand’s design, positioning, and communication style, establishing a clear creative and strategic foundation for all future lifecycle work.

2

Win-back flow implementation

The flow targets subscribers who have been inactive for 60 days. Previously missing from the account, it was designed to re-engage dormant customers and recover lost revenue by bringing them back into the buying cycle.

3

A/B testing across high-revenue flows

Continuous A/B testing on the highest revenue-driving flows, specifically the Welcome Flow and Checkout Abandonment Flow. Variables tested included subject lines, send timing and message delays, offer vs. no-offer strategies, and dynamic product blocks versus static imagery and category-based layouts.

4

Deliverability protection through sunset flow

A Sunset Flow improved deliverability by re-engaging inactive subscribers and removing consistently disengaged contacts, keeping the account healthy and optimized for sustainable growth.

5

Post-purchase & repeat purchase optimization

We redesigned the first-purchase experience and rebuilt repeat customer flows with new strategy, copy, and design to drive second- and third-purchase conversions.

Takeaways

What a lifecycle email strategy can do for your brand

Through a strategic combination of brand-aligned campaigns, high-impact flow optimization, and deliverability-first list management, this brand’s email ecosystem became a high-performing revenue channel.

By focusing on both immediate performance and long-term customer value, the brand not only reactivated dormant users but also significantly increased first-time and repeat purchases. This partnership reinforced the importance of a balanced lifecycle and campaign strategy in driving sustainable, scalable growth.
